## ARTIST BIO

Jake Singelais is a Maine-based photographer currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Art and Entrepreneurial Studies with a concentration in photography at the University of Southern Maine. His work focuses on portraiture, nightlife, and black-and-white photography, emphasizing atmosphere, precision, and emotional tone through intentional composition and lighting.

Beginning photography in Windsor, Vermont, Singelais developed his practice through photographing friends, urban environments, and commissioned work. His portfolio combines portrait, landscape, still life, and nightlife imagery while exploring human presence and visual storytelling. Alongside personal projects, he works professionally through portrait sessions, weddings, senior photography, and event documentation. He has also participated in pop-up shops throughout Portland and Gorham, using these spaces to connect with audiences and expand his creative practice.
